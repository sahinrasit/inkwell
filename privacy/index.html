Privacy Policy

Inkwell Scan · Last updated 13 October 2026

Inkwell Scan turns photos of handwritten notebook pages into Markdown. It is built so that your pages and notes stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Inkwell Scan has no account, no sign-up, no analytics or advertising SDKs and no tracking. The developer does not receive your pages, images, text or any usage data.

Processing on your device

Page straightening (VisionKit), handwriting recognition (Apple Vision) and the optional text cleanup (Apple Intelligence on-device Foundation Models, where available) all run locally on your iPhone or iPad. Your pages are stored inside the app on your device and are included in your device backup if you use one.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only to photograph pages you choose to scan. Photo library access is used only to import a page photo you pick, or to save a page image when you ask the app to.

Exports

When you export a note, it goes only where you send it: a folder you choose in the Files app (for example an Obsidian vault), the share sheet, or the clipboard. What happens there is governed by that app or service.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. To check whether you have unlocked the app, purchase status may be verified through RevenueCat, a purchase-validation service, using an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase history. This information is not linked to your identity, is not used for tracking, and never includes your pages or notes. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
