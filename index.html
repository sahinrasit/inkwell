Frequently asked

Does my handwriting leave my phone?
No. Recognition and the optional Apple Intelligence cleanup run on the device. There is no account and no server.
How do I save into my Obsidian vault?
Open a page, tap Export → Save to Obsidian Folder and pick your vault folder once in Files. Notes are saved to Inkwell/<Notebook>/<date>-p<page>.md with front matter. If you use Obsidian Sync, choose the vault folder that is on this device.
Some words are wrong.
Cursive accuracy depends on the handwriting. Open the Check tab: unsure lines are highlighted and tapping one shows it on the page, so you can fix it while looking at your writing.
How do I restore my purchase?
Settings → Restore Purchases, or the Restore link on the unlock screen.
Which languages work?
English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and other languages supported by Apple's text recognition. Turkish is read with Latin-script recognition plus a Turkish correction pass.
